About this tool

Spatial calculations use observer-relative three-dimensional geometry. WGS84 geographic coordinates are converted to ECEF and then to a local ENU frame. Version 1.0 supports arbitrary 3D triangles and planar or approximately planar simple polygons without holes. Terrain viewshed and external occlusion analysis remain outside the Version 1.0 scope.
